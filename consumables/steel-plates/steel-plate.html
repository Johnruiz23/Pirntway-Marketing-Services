Pad Printing Steel Plate

Winon Industrial Co., Ltd. — Dongguan, China

The Steel Plate is a precision-ground, hardened steel blank used to make pad printing plates (clichés). It is etched or engraved with your artwork and mounted on the plate carrier of the ink well or ink cup assembly. Plates are cut to size for your specific machine carrier.

ABOUT THIS PART

Choosing your steel plate

What is a pad printing steel plate

Steel plates (also called clichés) carry the printing image for pad printing. The image is recessed by etching so it holds ink, which the silicone pad lifts and transfers onto the part. Hardened, precision-ground steel gives long production runs and consistent detail.

Sizes & fitting

Plates are supplied to suit the plate carrier of your pad printing machine and the size of your artwork. Confirm the carrier type, plate length and width, and the image layout when ordering.

Order note

Tell us your machine model, artwork dimensions, and the number of printing positions. We will confirm the plate size, thickness, and etching parameters for your job.

SELECTION GUIDE

Choosing your silicone pad

Pad Shape

Choose the pad shape that best suits your printing requirements (refer to the pad shape chart). When recording the pad model, please note whether it is an ordinary pad or a pad plate.

Silicone Material

Pads are produced from non-toxic silicone. Winon selects from five representative raw materials:

  • Enhanced — red — German raw material: excellent ink transfer, good resilience, long service life, fatigue resistant, good solvent resistance.
  • Enhanced — blue — Italian raw material: better ink transfer and excellent solvent resistance.
  • Ordinary — yellow — Japanese raw material: the most widely used silicone raw material.
  • Standard — red — domestic R&D raw material with complete intellectual property; testing shows performance similar to the German raw material.
  • Anti-static — pink — eliminates static on the pad, improving ink take-up and transfer to reduce bubbles, pinholes and other printing defects.

Hardness

Hardness is measured with the Japanese TECLOCK GS-701N rubber hardness tester. Pads are divided into nine hardness levels:

  • Level 1: 1 – 3° (±2)
  • Level 2: 8 – 10° (±2)
  • Level 3: 13 – 15° (±2)
  • Level 4: 18 – 20° (±2)
  • Level 5: 23 – 25° (±2)
  • Level 6: 28 – 30° (±2)
  • Level 7: 33 – 35° (±2)
  • Level 8: 38 – 40° (±2)
  • Level 9: 43 – 45° (±2)

Pad Type & Surface

Pads are available as ordinary pads or pad plates, each in a smooth or matte finish. A smooth surface provides a higher finish and reproduces fine detail; a matte surface offers stronger ink pick-up and better ink transfer.

  • Ordinary pad — smooth surface
  • Ordinary pad — matte surface
  • Pad plate — smooth surface
  • Pad plate — matte surface

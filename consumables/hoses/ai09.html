TOGAWA PLASTIC HOSE

Takumi Air Hose

Air tool Hose

Togawa Industry Corporation — Kurobe, Toyama, Japan

Characterized by the extraordinary flexibility of the special resin. You may feel as if no hose is attached. Super flexible hose with light weight coupler! This hose can be used in a wide variety of applications such as pegger machines, air-tuckers, and air-spray guns. This hose may be well praised even by professional skilled workers. The couplers, made of aluminum, are characterized by the light weigh, resistance to damages, and increased removability and operability. In addition, the couplers are superior in versatility which can be used for a variety of applications ranging from in-factory air piping to hose connection with air tools.

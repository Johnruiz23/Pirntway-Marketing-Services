ABOUT THIS PART

Understanding these parts

What is a knife clamp

The knife clamp holds the doctor blade against the ink well so the blade stops off the plate surface evenly and cleanly. A correctly set clamp prevents ink build-up and ghosting on the print.

Sizes

Knife clamps are matched to the width of your ink well: 4-inch and 6-inch variants are available.

Order note

Confirm the ink well width and the blade you are using so the clamp matches your machine.

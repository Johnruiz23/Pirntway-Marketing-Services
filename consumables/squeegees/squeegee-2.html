ABOUT THIS PART

Understanding this part

What is a squeegee

The squeegee floods ink across the screen and then presses it through the open mesh onto the part in the print stroke. The condition and edge of the blade directly affect print quality.

Getting it right

Blade hardness, edge sharpness, and contact angle should be matched to the ink and mesh. Keep the blade edge free of nicks and set the squeegee parallel to the screen for even pressure.

Order note

Confirm the squeegee width you need and how it mounts to your machine head so we supply the correct style.

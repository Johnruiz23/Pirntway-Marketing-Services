Understanding this part

What is a squeegee clip

The squeegee clip locks the squeegee blade into the holder on the screen printing head. It keeps the blade square and evenly seated so pressure stays consistent along the full print width.

Order note

Confirm the squeegee width and holder type on your machine so the clip matches your set-up.

ABOUT THIS PART

Choosing your ink cup

What is an ink cup

The ink cup is a closed system that carries its own ink reservoir and doctor ring. It seals the ink inside, reducing solvent evaporation and contamination, and doses the ink onto the steel plate through the ring in a single pass.

Size

Ink cups are selected by the diameter of the doctor ring. The Winon pad part range includes Φ60, Φ86, Φ90, Φ135, and Φ142 mm cups. The cup must fit the ink cup holder and the plate width of your machine.

Metal or ceramic ring

Metal doctor rings are the standard choice for most inks and jobs, offering predictable wear and easy maintenance. This item is supplied with a metal ring.

Order note

Specify the cup diameter and ring material when ordering so we can confirm a drop-in fit for your machine.

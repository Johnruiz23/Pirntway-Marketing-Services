ABOUT THIS PART

Understanding this part

What is a screen frame

The screen frame carries the mesh at the correct tension so the stencil stays stable during printing. Frames are built to your print area and machine size, and the mesh is tensioned before the stencil is prepared.

Sizes & fitting

Frame dimensions must suit the screen frame clamps on your screen printing machine and leave clearance around the print area. Confirm the frame size and profile when ordering.

Order note

Tell us your machine model, the image size to print, and the mesh type you plan to use. We will confirm the frame dimensions and frame making service for your job.

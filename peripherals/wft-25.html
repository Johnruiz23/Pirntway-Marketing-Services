FLAME TREATMENT

WFT-25

Flame Treatment Machine

Winon Industrial Co., Ltd. — Dongguan, China

The Winon WFT-25 flame treatment machine pre-treats plastic containers by removing surface contamination and raising the surface energy of the material. This significantly improves ink adhesion, ensuring firm, durable printing on PP, PE, PET and PVC surfaces.

Technical Parameters

Machine TypeWFT-25
Bottle Diameter12 ~ 24 mm
Bottle Height20 ~ 300 mm
Burning GasLPG
Combustion GasCompressed air
Speed1800 ~ 3000 p/h
Dimensions1830 x 780 x 1540 mm
Net Weight245 kg

Application Areas

Flame surface treatment of PP, PE, PET and PVC plastic products of different shapes, such as buckets, cups, bottles and cans.

KEY FEATURES

Technical Features

Strong Ink Adhesion

High temperature flame treatment removes surface contamination and improves surface ink adhesion on plastics.

Flexible Cone Support

Universal cone support adapts to common bottle mouths, with special sizes available on request.

Stepless Speed Adjustment

Micro speed-regulating motor drive with stepless adjustment handles a wide range of workpiece shapes and sizes.

Fuel-Efficient Design

Advanced flame head and manifold design is unique and fuel-efficient for energy-saving operation.

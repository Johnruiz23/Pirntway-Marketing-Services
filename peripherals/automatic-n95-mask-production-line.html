N95 MASK PRODUCTION LINE

Automatic N95 Mask Production Line (WZP-MASKN95/Z)

Fully Automatic N95 Respirator Mask Line

Winon Industrial Co., Ltd. — Dongguan, China

The Winon automatic N95 mask production line (WZP-MASKN95/Z) manufactures N95 respirator masks in a fully automatic process. From material feeding and folding to ultrasonic bonding and ear-loop attachment, the line produces finished, packable N95 respirator masks at high throughput.

Screen Stretcher

Screen Stretcher for Frame Tensioning

Winon Industrial Co., Ltd. — Dongguan, China

The Winon screen stretcher is used to mount and tension printing mesh evenly onto screen frames. Uniform, controlled tension keeps the mesh flat and dimensionally stable, producing accurate, repeatable screens that are ready for coating and exposure.
